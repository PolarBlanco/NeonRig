PLUG IN

  1. Plug the guitar into an audio interface or a USB guitar cable (such as the Rocksmith Real Tone Cable). On a Scarlett: the instrument jack (input 2 on a Solo), INST on, and Direct Monitor OFF.
  2. Press START and allow microphone access. INPUT and OUTPUT on AUTO find the guitar and play out of the same box, or out of the computer's headphones/speakers for a cable with no output (Rocksmith). Plug things in or swap them any time; AUTO follows.
  3. Use wired headphones or speakers. Bluetooth adds too much delay to play along.
  4. Into your real amp: load Real Amp: Chorus + Reverb (CAB SIM off), take the Scarlett's LINE OUT L to the amp's input or FX return with a mono cable, Direct Monitor OFF, and start with the output low.
  5. Keep the input out of the red: on a Scarlett, its gain ring should glow green when you dig in. Fine-tune with GAIN here.
  6. Watch the little 1 / 2 meters. Choose the channel your guitar lights up (AUTO does this for you).

KILL THE NOISE

  • Hold the strings quiet and press LEARN NOISE. It finds your mains hum (50 or 60 Hz) and sets the GATE just above your hiss.
  • Buzz that comes and goes as you turn is from single-coil pickups: face away from monitors and dimmers.
  • Crackles or dropouts are the computer, not the guitar: set BUF to SAFE (or MAX).
  • Static even when nothing is plugged in usually means a ground loop: try the laptop on battery (charger unplugged).

PLAY

  • Knobs: drag up/down, or scroll. Hold Shift for fine moves; double-click resets.
  • Footswitch (bottom of each pedal): click to engage or bypass. The LED shows the state.
  • Move pedals: drag them by the body. Reorder the signal: drag the chips in SIGNAL CHAIN.
  • ARRANGE lines everything up in chain order. + ADD PEDAL adds more (doubles are fine).
  • The board saves itself. Use SAVE to keep named presets.

LATENCY

Browsers add some delay. Use Chrome or Edge, close other audio apps, and set the Scarlett to 48 kHz in your system sound settings. The readout in the top bar is the browser's own estimate.
